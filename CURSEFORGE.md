## Summary (one line, under 150 characters)

Folds the RestedXP guide steps and your quest log into one clean panel on the right, in a Blizzard or modern look.

## Description

**RXP Quest Panel** hides the RestedXP Guide window and redraws your current guide steps, together with your quest log, in one tidy panel on the right side of the screen. The RestedXP arrow and everything else in RestedXP stay as they are.

*Unofficial companion addon. Not made by or affiliated with RestedXP. Requires the RestedXP Guide addon.*

### Features

- **One panel:** guide steps on top, tracked quests below, in the spot where the quest tracker usually sits.
- **Two looks:** Blizzard Native (gold headers, page-turn arrows, the game's own progress bars) or Waypoint Rail (a dark panel with a line down the side marking your current step).
- **First-time setup:** drag the panel where you want it, set its largest size and pick a look, with the panel as a live preview.
- **Look ahead without losing your place:** the < and > arrows let you peek at other steps while RestedXP stays on your current one.
- **Follows quest tracking:** shows the quests you track. Shift-click a quest to track it, or show every quest with one click.
- **Right-click menus:** right-click a header for the look, quest filters, lock and setup; right-click a quest to track, open, map, share or abandon it.
- **Options page:** Esc > Options > AddOns > RXP Quest Panel, or the addon button by the minimap.
- **Flight timer** sits inside the panel under the guide name while you fly.
- Sizes itself to its content and only grows as tall as you allow.

### Commands

`/rxpq options` opens the Options page. `/rxpq setup` reopens setup. `/rxpq toggle` switches back to RestedXP's own window. `/rxpq` lists the rest.
